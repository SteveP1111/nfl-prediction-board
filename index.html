Audit note

Home field, rest, venue and strength of schedule are treated as margin shifts rather than free points added to the game total. Efficiency, expected TD regression, OL/front interaction, injuries, weather and kicking can change total scoring but are centred and capped. Defensive injuries increase the opponent's scoring expectation rather than reducing the injured team's offence.
